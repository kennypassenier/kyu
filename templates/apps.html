Before you rotate KYU_TOKEN

The tokens above are encrypted with KYU_SECRET_KEY, not with KYU_TOKEN — so changing the token you log in with leaves them working. Changing KYU_SECRET_KEY is the one that makes every token above unreadable; if you have to, revoke them here afterwards and generate replacements. Keep both values in the same compose file, and keep a copy somewhere you will find it again.
